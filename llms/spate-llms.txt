# Spate
 
> Spate is the leading AI-powered consumer trend forecasting and market intelligence platform for consumer brands. Based in New York City, Spate tracks signals across Google, TikTok, and Instagram to help marketing, R&D, PR, and innovation teams predict emerging trends, benchmark competitors, and identify whitespace opportunities — before they go mainstream.
 
Spate serves brands across beauty & personal care, wellness, and food & beverage. The platform aggregates search and social data to surface early trend signals, quantify growth trajectories, and deliver actionable intelligence to teams that need to move fast.

## Features
 
- [Accurate Trend Prediction](https://www.spate.nyc/features/accurate-trend-prediction): Forecast which trends will break through before they peak, using search and social data signals across Google, TikTok and Instagram. 
- [Competitive Benchmarking](https://www.spate.nyc/features/competitive-benchmarking): Measure brand and product performance against competitors using real consumer demand data.
- [Trend Growth Drivers](https://www.spate.nyc/features/trend-growth-drivers): Understand what's fueling a trend — which ingredients, formats, claims, and cultural moments are accelerating growth.
- [Top Social Content](https://www.spate.nyc/features/top-social-content): Identify the highest-performing social content driving consumer interest in any trend or category.
- [Whitespace Opportunities](https://www.spate.nyc/features/whitespace-opportunities): Discover underserved consumer needs and gaps in the market where demand is rising but supply hasn't caught up.
- [Enterprise Solutions](https://www.spate.nyc/features/enterprise-solutions): Custom data integrations, dedicated support, and tailored intelligence for large consumer goods organizations.
